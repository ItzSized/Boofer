Why Boodev?

Everything your AI agents do, at a glance.

No more jumping between 4 terminal windows and your IDE to know whether your agent is done or waiting for a confirmation.

📊

Progress & time left

Shows the project, your initial request, the current step ("Step 2/5 · Writing the tests — 70%"), the number of actions, the changed files and a smart estimate of the time left.

🔐

Approvals without switching screens

When Claude Code asks for permission to run a command or edit a file, a card appears under the ghost with Allow / Always / Deny.

🪟

Multi-session & window tracking

Each session has its own color and tab. Boodev automatically detects the project open in the window in front (Antigravity, VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf).

👀

A real living companion

Its eyes follow your cursor, it waves on hover, loves being petted (with little hearts and giggles), can follow your mouse around the screen and falls asleep when you take a break.

🔔

Web Audio sounds & night mode

Sounds synthesized in real time with Web Audio (done arpeggio, soft alert, little giggles) without any heavy MP3 file, and a one-click "Do not disturb" mode.

👻

Transparent & Click-Through

Clicks go through every transparent area around the ghost: you can click and select your code right next to it without ever being blocked.

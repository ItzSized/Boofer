            🧹 Compact earlier

            With every message, Claude rereads the whole session context: that's where almost all your tokens go. Keeping the context smaller makes each message cheaper. It just forgets old details sooner.

            Compact at:

            "Normal" = when the context is full (up to 1M tokens for Opus 5.5).

            ✍️ Short answers

            Asks Claude to get to the point: no recap, no code copied for nothing. Adds 4 lines to ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md, removed if you untick it.

            🔁 Reprompt

            Write your request normally: Boofer rewrites it as "AI code", just for the AI. Each part goes under a short tag: #do the steps (separated by >), #not what not to do, #must what must stay true, #out the expected answer, #ctx the context. Unreadable for a human, but the AI gets it right away and reads fewer tokens.

            Anywhere: copy your prompt, Ctrl+Alt+P, then paste the AI version.

            ✨ Ctrl+Alt+P with Haiku

            Smarter rewrite (≈ 5 s) instead of the instant local compression.

            Local compression: free and instant. Haiku: smarter (may switch to English, shorter), but it runs with your Claude Code account, so it costs a tiny bit. Your prompt is only a few dozen tokens next to the context: the two options above save much more.
